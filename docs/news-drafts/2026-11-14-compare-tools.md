# Smarter Pool Comparison: Volume Data and a Pick-and-Compare Tool

Choosing between 48 factory pool sizes gets easier with two upgrades to our [compare page](https://felizbay.com/products/compare/).

## Estimated volume for every factory size

The interactive factory table now includes an **Est. volume (L)** column — the geometric full-fill capacity computed from each pool's published dimensions. A Ø 4 m pool with 1.2 m walls, for example, holds roughly 13,000 litres at capacity (about 13 metric tons of water), which is the number that matters for heating-cost planning, filter-pump sizing and deck-load checks. As always, real fills stop at the marked fill line, and the table says so.

The retail comparison table also gained **Dimensions** and **Water Capacity** rows, plus a **Heater** row covering the optional external heater for the round SPA tub.

## Pick 2–4 and compare side by side

Tick the checkbox on any rows of the 48-SKU factory table and press **Compare selected**: a side-by-side panel lines up your picks across seven attributes — item number, series, size, wall, estimated volume, packed weight and FOB unit price — with a one-click jump to request a wholesale quotation. Selection is capped at four models to keep the comparison readable.

## Pool Finder now names exact models

The [Find Your Pool](https://felizbay.com/find-your-pool/) advisor has also been upgraded: each business scenario now lists the exact factory models that fit your space, with sizes, wall heights and FOB prices — instead of a generic size band. Try the business path with your available area and see the fitting models named one by one.

All comparison data comes from the same single source — our [factory specs and price list](https://felizbay.com/products/specs/) — so the numbers you compare are the numbers we quote.
