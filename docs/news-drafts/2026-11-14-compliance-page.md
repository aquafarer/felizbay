# New Compliance & Documentation Page

We have published a dedicated [Compliance & Documentation](https://felizbay.com/compliance/) page that separates the certifications we hold into four clearly-labelled categories — because "certified" means very different things depending on what is actually being certified.

## The four claim categories

1. **Product conformity** — CE marking for the European market and EN 16582-1, the standard for residential domestic swimming pools.
2. **Design-reference standards** — ASTM F2666 as the US design reference for our residential-family pools. A design reference is not a product certification, and the page says so explicitly.
3. **Material compliance** — REACH, RoHS and California Proposition 65 screening for the PVC and fabric materials, plus construction test bases such as ASTM D751 and accelerated UV weathering (ASTM G154).
4. **Factory management certification** — ISO 9001:2015 (SGS-audited) for the quality management system and BSCI (amfori) for social compliance.

## What we do not claim

The page includes a "What We Do Not Claim" section: residential-pool standards do not transfer to public or commercial pool installations, certificates are provided per order in their current version rather than published with unverified expiry dates, and third-party test reports are shared under NDA with business partners.

For business buyers, the page explains which documents accompany which kind of claim, and how to request the current version of any certificate or report for a specific order.

The new page is linked from [Quality Control](https://felizbay.com/quality-control/) and listed in our [B2B resources](https://felizbay.com/llms.txt).
