# New Product: Premium Drop Stitch Dog Pool

We have added a sixth product page to the site: the [Premium Drop Stitch Inflatable Dog Pool](https://felizbay.com/products/drop-stitch-dog-pool/).

## What it is

A dedicated dog pool, not a repurposed family model. A generous **3.0 m round basin** with a **0.5 m low rim** — easy step-in for dogs — and a **10 cm specified drop-stitch wall** that inflates firm at working pressure. No sagging air-ring skin, no wobbly sides: the same high-pressure construction as our family pools, sized for villa gardens and private backyards where larger breeds and multi-pet households need room to splash.

## What is on the page

- Full specification table (shape, wall material, dimensions, construction, application)
- A 6-step setup guide and package contents
- Supervision and safety rules for dogs around water — pets are never left unattended, and the pool is a way to cool off, not a substitute for fresh drinking water
- Product conformity documentation, claim-separated from the spec table
- Private-label and wholesale information for pet brands, distributors, and pet hotels

## For partners

Pet brands, distributors, and retailers can take this model as a private-label pet-pool line: Felizbay supports customized branding and packaging. The page runs in inquiry mode — partners and owners request a quotation through the [contact form](https://felizbay.com/contact/).

Also relevant for dog owners: our existing [Pools for Pet Families](https://felizbay.com/pool-ideas/pools-for-pet-families/) guide covers claw-resistance limits, cleaning, and supervision rules.
