# Full-Process Destructive Test Reports Published

Every Felizbay pool is pressure-tested before it ships — that is routine QC. To show how much margin sits between normal operation and failure, we went further and tested a complete pool to destruction, stage by stage. The four resulting reports are now published in full at [Destructive Testing Reports](https://felizbay.com/destructive-testing/).

## What was tested

One representative specification was tested end to end: a 400 × 300 × 120 cm drop-stitch pool with 20 cm walls, joined by high-frequency/hot-air welding plus two-component polyurethane cold adhesive (reports RPT-FB-QC-20261113-S1/S2/S3/FINAL, test date 2026-11-13).

## The three stages

1. **Raw material & fabric** — 1000D drop-stitch space fabric sampled from production material: 120 °C hot-air pre-peel, 180° peel tensile testing, cohesive-failure review of the separated faces, and an 8.0 PSI pressurized single-point puncture test.
2. **Welding & adhesive process limits** — a 48-hour high-temperature creep measurement on the composite welded-and-glued edge, then a step-by-step overpressure burst inside a blast cage. The structure failed at **25.4 PSI — 3.18× the 8 PSI maximum working pressure**.
3. **Full-load hydrostatic & dynamic impact** — a 110% overload fill of **10,810 L held for 72 hours** (maximum outward wall lean 5.6°), a 150 kg seat compression, **500 pendulum impacts**, and a valve flange torque test on the 20 cm wall.

The final conclusion report consolidates all 8 destructive verification metrics, assesses the safety margins across design, material and process, and records the mass-production release approval.

## How to read the results

The page includes a "How to Read These Results" section: these are factory destructive type-tests measuring safety margins under controlled overload — not a product certification (conformity documentation is listed separately on the [compliance page](https://felizbay.com/compliance/)). The burst margin is headroom, not an operating target: daily use stays at the recommended 4–6 PSI, and the pressure-relief valve keeps working pressure in range automatically.

All four PDF reports are downloadable from the page, which is linked from [Quality Control](https://felizbay.com/quality-control/) and the [full specifications](https://felizbay.com/products/specs/) pressure section.
