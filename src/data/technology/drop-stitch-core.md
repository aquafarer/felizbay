Drop-stitch core construction uses thousands of polyester threads connecting the top and bottom PVC layers. When inflated to the recommended 4–5 PSI operating pressure, these threads lock the two layers at a fixed distance, creating a rigid, flat structure that behaves like a rigid-frame pool rather than a soft vinyl inflatable.

Every Felizbay drop-stitch pool runs at the same pressure standard: 4–5 PSI recommended operating pressure, a pressure-relief valve on the air chamber side walls that automatically releases above 6 PSI, and an 8 PSI maximum structural tolerance. The 6 PSI and 8 PSI figures are safety ratings verified by a 24–48 hour pressure-drop leak test at the factory — they are never inflation targets.

This technology is what lets a Felizbay pool hold flat, rigid walls all season and still fold into a storage bag when the season ends.
