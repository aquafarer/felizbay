## Premium Drop Stitch Inflatable Dog Pool

The Felizbay Premium Drop Stitch Inflatable Dog Pool turns a villa garden or private backyard into a private water-play spot made for dogs. Instead of a wobbly ring pool, its wall is built with high-pressure drop stitch construction — thousands of internal threads lock the two fabric layers together, so the 10 cm wall inflates firm and keeps a clean, structured look that suits premium homes. The generous 3-meter round basin leaves plenty of room for larger breeds and multi-pet households to splash, play, and cool off, subject to the dogs' size and available swimming space.

## Why Drop Stitch for a Dog Pool

- **Firm, structured wall** — the 10 cm specified thickness inflates into a rigid, self-supporting wall with a clean, modern appearance.
- **Spacious 3-meter basin** — a full 3.0 m diameter gives dogs room to splash and play; ideal for larger breeds and multi-pet households.
- **Premium look for premium homes** — designed for pet-loving families who want pet equipment that matches a well-kept villa garden.
- **Pack-away storage** — drain, deflate, and store the pool compactly at the end of the season.

## Where the Dog Pool Fits

- Villa gardens and private yards
- Outdoor pet recreation areas
- Dog owners with spacious gardens
- Premium pet-friendly homes
- Pet hotels and professional pet facilities

When temperatures rise, dogs need access to fresh drinking water, shade, and appropriate ways to stay cool. A private dog pool creates a fun water-play experience while giving owners a convenient way to supervise their pets outdoors — no crowded public swimming areas, no unnecessary trips.
