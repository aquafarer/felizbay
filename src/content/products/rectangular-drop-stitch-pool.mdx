## Rectangular Inflatable Drop Stitch Swimming Pool

The Felizbay rectangular inflatable drop stitch swimming pool turns any backyard into a private swim spot. Unlike cheap ring pools, its walls are built from double-layer PVC drop stitch fabric — thousands of internal threads lock the top and bottom layers together, so the pool inflates rock-hard at 8–10 PSI and behaves like a rigid-frame pool while still packing into a storage bag when the season ends.

## Why Drop Stitch

- **Rigid, flat walls** — the pool keeps its exact shape when filled; no bulging, no sagging.
- **High-pressure rated** — engineered for 8–10 PSI, far above ordinary inflatable pools.
- **Fast setup** — inflate with the included electric pump in about 10 minutes; fill with a garden hose.
- **Compact storage** — drain, deflate, roll up, and store in a closet or garage.
- **Durable exterior** — reinforced PVC layers resist punctures, UV exposure, and daily family use.

## Perfect For

- Family backyard swimming and summer play
- Swim training for kids in a controlled, private pool
- Holiday homes and rental properties without permanent pools
- Water fitness, aqua-aerobics, and cooling off on hot days
