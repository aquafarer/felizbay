## Round Drop-Stitch Soaking Pool with Built-In Seats

The Felizbay round soaking pool is the compact member of our SPA tub family — a small-footprint soaking pool that warms up quickly with an external heater (not included) and fits private courtyards, load-rated balconies, and indoor spa corners. Built-in integrated inflatable seats and a 5 mm insulated thermal bottom base come standard, and the drop stitch walls (8/10/15 cm profiles) inflate rigid at the recommended 4–5 PSI operating pressure.

Like every Felizbay drop stitch product it packs down to a fraction of its inflated size, slashing shipping and storage costs compared with rigid acrylic or fiberglass tubs.

## Key Features

- **Compact soaking design** — small footprint, quick warm-up with an external heater (sold separately), ideal for private courtyards and load-rated balconies.
- **Built-in integrated inflatable seats** — comfortable seating for daily soaks and cold-plunge recovery.
- **5 mm insulated thermal bottom** — retains heat and reduces heating energy costs.
- **Rigid drop stitch walls** — 8/10/15 cm profiles in three diameters (1.8 m / 2.0 m / 2.3 m).
- **System compatibility** — connects to heating units, sand filter pumps, inline disinfection, and air bubble massage systems.

Inflation pressure safety: inflate only to the recommended 4–5 PSI operating pressure. The 6 PSI factory QC test and pressure-relief valve release point and the 8 PSI maximum pressure tolerance are safety ratings, not inflation targets — always follow the product manual and valve markings.
