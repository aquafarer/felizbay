## Round Drop-Stitch Soaking Pool with Built-In Seats

The Felizbay round soaking pool is the compact member of our SPA tub family — a small-footprint soaking pool that pairs with an external heater (not included) and fits private courtyards, load-rated balconies, and indoor spa corners. This page sells the fixed online set: the Ø 2 × 0.8 m model (factory item RS2018) with built-in integrated inflatable seats, a 5 mm insulated thermal bottom base, and a 10 cm drop stitch wall that inflates rigid at the recommended 4–6 PSI operating pressure.

Like every Felizbay drop stitch product it packs down to a fraction of its inflated size, slashing shipping and storage costs compared with rigid acrylic or fiberglass tubs.

## Key Features

- **Compact soaking design** — small footprint, ideal for private courtyards and load-rated balconies; the external heater is sold separately.
- **Built-in integrated inflatable seats** — comfortable seating for daily soaks and cold plunges.
- **5 mm insulated thermal bottom** — retains heat between soaks.
- **Rigid drop stitch walls** — 10 cm profile on the Ø 2 × 0.8 m retail set (item RS2018).
- **System compatibility** — connects to heating units, sand filter pumps, inline disinfection, and air bubble massage systems.

Inflation pressure safety: inflate only to the recommended 4–6 PSI operating pressure. The 6 PSI factory QC test and pressure-relief valve release point and the 8 PSI maximum pressure tolerance are factory safety ratings — always follow the product manual and valve markings.
