## Round Grey Inflatable Drop Stitch Swimming Pool

The Felizbay round grey pool is our premium take on the inflatable swimming pool. The understated grey finish suits modern homes, and the double-layer drop stitch walls inflate rigid at 4–5 PSI, giving you a true rigid pool that still folds into a bag at the end of summer.

## Key Features

- **Premium grey finish** — a modern look that blends with contemporary patios and gardens.
- **Rigid drop stitch walls** — high-pressure rated construction keeps the pool round, stable, and strong.
- **Optional fitted cover** — a matching cover keeps leaves and debris out and the water warm.
- **Effortless seasonal use** — inflate in about 10 minutes, drain, dry, and store flat.
- **Complete package** — electric air pump, repair kit, and storage bag included.

Inflation pressure safety: inflate only to the recommended 4–5 PSI operating pressure. The 6 PSI pressure-relief valve release point and the 8 PSI maximum pressure tolerance are safety ratings, not inflation targets — always follow the product manual and valve markings.
